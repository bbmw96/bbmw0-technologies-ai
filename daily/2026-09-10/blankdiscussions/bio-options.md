## Profile picture

`profile-picture.svg`, in this same folder: a plain eight-pointed geometric star (a khatam, the classic non-figurative Islamic geometric motif), not a crescent moon, so it does not look like every other generic Islamic content account. Uses the same emerald-and-gold palette as the account's primary theme, kept fixed as the account's identity mark even as individual post colours rotate.

It is an SVG, not a PNG or JPG, and Instagram requires a raster image for profile photos. To convert it: open the file in any web browser (double-click it), then use the browser's save/export or a screenshot at 1080x1080 to produce a PNG, or open it in any image editor (Photoshop, GIMP, Preview, even Word) and export as PNG. No paid AI image generation was used, per the project's standing policy of not spending on generation without asking first.
